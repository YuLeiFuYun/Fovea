Media files under `Examples/FoveaWorkbenchApp/FoveaWorkbench/Resources/LocalMedia`
are not relicensed under MIT. They are limited by project policy to CC0 or public-domain
works. The authoritative per-file title, author, source URL, license name, license URL,

`Examples/FoveaWorkbenchApp/FoveaWorkbench/Resources/workbench-media-catalog.json`.

Generated benchmark resources are excluded from version control and must be recreated
from their source manifests. No private user media is part of the repository.
